# kku-hackathon-project

## What it does
A retro-arcade memory match game. Flip cards two at a time to find matching icon pairs. Tracks your moves and time, and keeps a local top-5 leaderboard.

## Who it is for
Anyone who wants a quick, fun brain game — no install, no sign-up, just open and play.

## Needs
Just a web browser. No accounts, no server, no internet connection required after download.

## How to run it
Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. Click "NEW GAME" to shuffle the cards and play again anytime.

## Try it with the sample data
On first open, the leaderboard shows built-in made-up example scores. Click "LOAD EXAMPLE" anytime to reset the leaderboard back to that example data.
